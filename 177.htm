McNeely vs. Tyson
September 12, 1995

The Tropicana Hotel & Casino
Las Vegas, Nevada

LAS VEGAS, Nev. - Greetings boxing fans! I have been drinking steadily all day, poolside, getting in the right frame of mind for tonights "Dud of the Decade" fight between Mike Tyson and whats his name McNeely.

I am in between scotchs and wanted to check in with my bookie. I have been trading black lies with a bunch of Teamsters from Jersey who are cousins of McNeely. They are extremely obnoxious and fight violently among themselves, calling each other, "stoooopids" and saying "gid ouda hea, you no good bastuds!" They have bet heavily on their favorite relative and ran all the descent people out of the bar.

Anyway, my limo is here along with Don Johnson, Gary Busey and an assortment of hangar-ons. We are on our way to the arena to sit in our very expensive seats (courtesy of Don King) and drink heavily and scream incoherent gibberish at the peasants sitting behind us. Gotta go,... Busey has passed out and we can't get a pulse. Adios!!!

From The Deep End
Jack Lono
