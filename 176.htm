Jack's Bio
Sunset
February 28, 1996

Lake Travis, Austin Texas

AUSTIN, Tx - My name is Jack Lono. I was born on the North Shore of Ohau, Hawaii under the shadow of Makaapu Ridge, a jump off point for hang gliders, parachutists and disillusioned journalists. I am the latter. Having traveled the world in search of truth as a working member of the world press and finding only the wretched stench of human failure, I have reevaluated my life and become a Public Relations Specialist. A sort of P.R. Czar if you will. The liquor flows more freely, the women are more adept, the accomodations are cleaner and the cha- ching factor is, well, a factor.

I attended Baine Prep in Connecticut, Vassar in New York and eventually joined the merchant marines to see the world and evade massive amounts of credit card debt. From there I went to work for the Associated Press as a stringer in New York. Finally, I was fired for making up my stories and calling them in from my favorite bar stool at Blackies Tavern in lower Manhattan.

Currently, I am living on a luxurious houseboat on this beautiful lake about thirty miles from the state capitol of Texas. I have the most up-to-date satellite communications up-link technology for my reports to go out on and I am currently in negotiations with CNN and Fox for my very own show called Your Next.

Basically, it's about me, covering certain current events taking place around the world where I report on what is taking place and what effect it is having on you and your universe. Simple, basic and to the point. No bullshit. Just straight, pigheaded, slanted, opiniated, rhetoric, on the state of affairs of this sorry ass existence we call humankind. All, from my point of view of course. Until then, you can catch me right here when I have something worthwhile to report. I hope you can drop by for a hit or two.

I must go now, the ice has melted from my multi-colored, Caribbean rum drink and the lake patrol is trying to board my vessel. I accidently rammed their police issue Boston Whaler while I went into the parlor to put on a White Zombie CD, which in turn jettisoned overboard the very expensive, Bose stereo speakers that were precariously perched on the roof of my floating temple of decadence, all the while, I was fireing my flare gun at passing, unsuspecting pleasure boaters. If you happen to be in the neighborhood, please come aboard for a cocktail sometime. Things usually start kicking around noon. You can't miss this magnificent beast of a pleasure craft. At seventy two feet long, I have the largest floating palace on the lake. I am the big blue monster, named after my favorite dancer, KITTY BIMBO. See you at the bar!

From The Deep End,
Jack Lono
